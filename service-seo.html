Every Dimension of SEO, Covered.

Ranking on page one requires excellence across technical, on-page, off-page, and content disciplines simultaneously. I cover all of them — in a coordinated strategy aligned to your commercial goals.

Technical SEO Audit

A deep crawl of your entire site examining indexability, crawl budget, duplicate content, canonical tags, redirect chains, hreflang, XML sitemaps, robots.txt, and structured data — with a prioritised remediation plan.

Keyword Research & Strategy

Identifying the exact terms your ideal customers use at every stage of the buying journey — from informational queries to high-intent transactional searches — then mapping them to a clear site architecture.

On-Page Optimisation

Optimising every on-page signal that influences rankings — title tags, meta descriptions, heading hierarchy, content depth, internal linking structure, image alt attributes, and URL patterns — applied systematically across your site.

Local SEO

Dominating local search results through Google Business Profile optimisation, local citation building, NAP consistency, local keyword targeting, and review strategy — essential for businesses serving Weston-super-Mare, Bristol, and Somerset.

Link Building & Digital PR

Earning high-authority backlinks through genuine digital PR, strategic outreach, and content-led link acquisition — building the domain authority that underpins long-term ranking performance without shortcuts that carry algorithmic risk.

SEO Content Strategy & Copywriting

Creating content that satisfies both search intent and the E-E-A-T quality signals Google rewards — authoritative, well-structured, and genuinely useful articles, landing pages, and service copy that ranks and converts.

Schema Markup & Structured Data

Implementing JSON-LD schema across all relevant page types — Organisation, LocalBusiness, Article, FAQPage, Product, BreadcrumbList, and more — to unlock rich results and provide clearer signals to search engines.

Core Web Vitals & Page Speed

Diagnosing and resolving LCP, INP, and CLS issues that affect both your Google rankings and user experience — image optimisation, render-blocking resource elimination, server response improvements, and layout stability fixes.

International SEO & Hreflang

Targeting multiple countries or languages with correct hreflang implementation, geotargeting configuration, international site architecture strategy, and country-specific keyword research to ensure the right audience finds the right content.

Competitor & Gap Analysis

A systematic analysis of what your top-ranking competitors are doing — their keyword footprint, backlink profiles, content strategy, and technical setup — to identify the gaps you can exploit and the opportunities they have missed.

SEO Reporting & Search Console Management

Monthly reporting tied to commercial KPIs — organic traffic, keyword rankings, click-through rates, and conversions — with full Google Search Console management, crawl error monitoring, and clear next-step recommendations.

E-E-A-T & Authority Building

Strengthening your site's Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness signals — author credibility, about pages, editorial standards, and third-party mentions — to satisfy Google's quality rater guidelines.

My Process

A Methodical Approach. Compounding Returns.

SEO is not a one-time task — it is an ongoing programme of continuous improvement. I build sustainable momentum through disciplined, evidence-based execution.

01

Discovery & Audit

A full technical crawl, competitor analysis, and keyword opportunity assessment. I establish where you stand today — rankings, traffic, authority, and technical health — and quantify exactly what is holding you back.

02

Strategy & Keyword Mapping

A documented SEO strategy with prioritised keyword targets, content gaps, technical fixes, and a link acquisition plan — all aligned to your commercial objectives and realistic ranking timelines.

03

Technical Remediation

Resolving the foundational issues that prevent your site from being properly crawled and indexed — redirect chains, duplicate content, slow page speed, broken internal links, and missing structured data — before any content or link work begins.

04

On-Page & Content Optimisation

Systematically optimising existing pages and creating new content to target priority keywords — with search intent at the centre of every decision, ensuring Google understands what each page is about and who it is for.

05

Off-Page & Authority Growth

Building a consistent pipeline of high-quality backlinks through digital PR campaigns, content outreach, and strategic partnerships — growing your domain authority in a sustainable, algorithm-safe way.

06

Monitor, Report & Iterate

Monthly performance reviews with clear reporting on rankings, traffic, and conversions. The strategy adapts as Google updates, competitors shift, and new opportunities emerge — ensuring your SEO programme stays ahead.
